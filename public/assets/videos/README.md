# PrismLine Roadmap Background Video

To customize the background video for the Roadmap section:
1. Generate or download an MP4 video (e.g. from Runway Gen-4, Sora, Kling, or Pexels).
   - Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1280x720 (720p).
   - Theme: Dark luxury, moody abstract waves, cybernetic lines, metallic textures, or automotive night atmosphere.
   - Codec: H.264 / MP4 (optimized for web, under 8-15 MB).
2. Save the file as:
   `public/assets/videos/roadmap-bg.mp4`

When placed here, it will automatically loop in the background of the Roadmap section with smooth parallax scrolling and ambient lighting overlays!
